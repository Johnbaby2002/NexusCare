What is an EHR?

An Electronic Health Record (EHR) is a digital chart that supports clinical workflows by storing demographics, symptoms, history, vitals, allergies, labs, imaging references, and billing-related data.

Security & Privacy
  • Authentication: doctor login with password hashing
  • Authorization: each doctor sees only their own patients
  • Extendable with audit logs
Project Fields
  • Inputs: name, weight, height
  • Symptoms: datalist autocomplete + free typing
  • Checkbox: smoker, visited
  • Radio: gender
  • Date + datepicker: DOB and visit date
  • Textarea: other allergies / notes
  • Image upload: radiology attachment
