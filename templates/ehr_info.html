EHR Info

What data NexusCare stores and how the record lifecycle works.

Electronic Health Record

Patient record model

In NexusCare, an EHR is handled as a practical clinical record: demographics + visit scheduling + symptoms and notes, with optional imaging upload. The system is doctor-scoped, so each doctor sees only their own patient list.

Demographics
Name, gender, date of birth, and derived age are used across the dashboard and patient list views.
Vitals & personal stats
Weight (and height when available) are stored in the patient record and shown in the patient details modal.
Scheduling
Visit date and visit time drive the appointment buckets on the dashboard and the Upcoming vs Visited split.
Clinical notes
Symptoms are displayed prominently for fast triage, and diagnosis / SOAP-style notes can be captured during updates.
Allergies & risk flags
Allergies are captured through a structured selection plus an optional free-text add-on, which keeps common entries fast while still allowing realistic edge cases.
Radiology image support
A patient record can include an uploaded radiology image (stored in the uploads folder and referenced in the database), which keeps the project closer to real EHR workflows.

Record lifecycle

The workflow is built around fast CRUD actions. A doctor can create a patient record, read it from lists and the patient details modal, update it through the edit form, and delete it when needed. The visited status controls whether a record appears in Upcoming or Visited, and the dashboard uses the same scheduling data to show appointments by date buckets.

Create
Add Patient captures demographics, scheduling, symptoms/notes, allergies, and optional imaging.
Read
Patient lists + a click-to-open modal provide instant access without leaving the current page.
Update
Edit Patient updates medical and scheduling data, including visit status and clinical notes.
Delete
Deleting removes the record from the doctor’s scope in the database.

Revisit scheduling

NexusCare supports revisit scheduling as a clean continuation of care. Instead of overwriting a completed visit, the system creates a new upcoming encounter based on the same patient information and places it back into the appointment pipeline. This keeps history intact while making follow-ups feel natural in the workflow.
